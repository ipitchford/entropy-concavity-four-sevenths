# The boundary q = 4/7: what is known

- **Not decided:** whether 4/7 belongs to the local-concavity set C. So C is (0, 4/7) or (0, 4/7].
- **Why the Theorem A proof stops:** at q = 4/7 the interval q < s < b(q/2) used in Section I.4 is empty. Lemma I.2 is
  undecided at its endpoint s = b(r) (Proposition S proves failure only for s > b(r)), and the extra smoothing margin
  s - q vanishes, which the derivative bounds of Section I.3 need.
- **Why Luo's construction does not give a counterexample there:** Proposition E shows that, at q = 4/7, Luo's
  moment-corrected family (any amplitudes eps_n -> 0 with eps_n n^(1/4) -> 0) has strictly negative entropy curvature on a
  fixed neighbourhood of t = 1/2. Negativity away from t = 1/2 is not proved. By Corollary S.5(4), no Hermite family
  h_{n,a} has the exponential gap between quadratic and linear terms that drives Theorem B when q <= 4/7.
- **What would settle it:** for membership, a derivative estimate for the quadratic term directly against ||N L||,
  without extra smoothing; for non-membership, a perturbation family whose quadratic interaction beats the linear term
  without an exponential gap. See Discussion D.5 of the paper.
